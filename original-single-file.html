Water-quality outputs are screening proxies, not direct concentrations in µg/L chlorophyll-a, NTU, or mg/L suspended solids. Results depend on atmospheric correction, water depth, bottom reflectance, CDOM, sun glint, adjacency effects, and local conditions. Validate against field measurements before quantitative or compliance use. Red-edge bands are 20 m; SWIR-based indices are effectively limited by 20 m input data.

Polygon summary statistics
Draw a polygon on the map after loading imagery to calculate pixel statistics for that area.

Statistics use valid cell centres inside the polygon on the app’s analysis grid, which is capped at 640 cells per side. Counts are not native-resolution Sentinel-2 pixel totals. Values summarize the selected index (or change layer), not a calibrated concentration unless you fit and validate a local model.

Choose OPTRAM and draw a polygon to assess relative wetness across the loaded individual acquisitions.

Persistence is the proportion of currently loaded individual acquisitions when the polygon's mean OPTRAM value meets the selected relative threshold. If Max Scenes subsampling is on, only those sampled acquisitions are assessed. It is not evidence of groundwater level, subsurface drainage, or foundation risk.

OPTRAM calibration diagnostics (internal consistency, not independent validation)

The chart shows the empirical dry/wet STR percentiles fitted within the selected AOI and loaded dates. It can reveal a weak or narrow feature-space fit, but without field measurements it cannot establish absolute soil-moisture accuracy.

Seasonal Anomaly Detection (Sentinel-2 Only)

Compare the selected acquisition with clear observations from other years at a similar time of year. Anomaly scores are robust, internally derived departures from the historical median—not probabilities, ground truth, or direct measurements. Use Individual Dates and a multi-year date range; cloud/shadow/snow masking is always enabled for this analysis.

Sentinel-1 Radar Explorer

Uses the same drawn rectangle and date range as Sentinel-2. Searches the public STAC metadata catalogue for Sentinel-1 RTC items. Raster access depends on the provider: if the asset-signing step returns HTTP 401/403, the item is discoverable but not readable by this no-login HTML app. Global Sentinel-1 GRD is also indexed publicly, but those source assets are requester-pays and are not a valid substitute for calibrated/terrain-corrected backscatter in this browser-only workflow. Radar backscatter is not a direct soil-moisture measurement and responds to vegetation, roughness, incidence angle, orbit and geometry.

Fallback: load downloaded Sentinel-1 RTC GeoTIFFs locally

Use this when remote RTC assets are not accessible without an account. Choose radiometrically calibrated, terrain-corrected Sentinel-1 RTC COGs (such as Gamma0/Sigma0), not raw GRD digital numbers. Files are read locally in this browser and are not uploaded.
