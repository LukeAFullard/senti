Air Quality Remote Sensing Test App & Local Area Viewer

Interactive validation suite for js/11-air-quality-module.js with interactive Local Area Selector, atmospheric mapping, raster simulation, and Sentinel-5P unit conversion.

Looking to view live satellite air quality in your local area? Use Section 1 below to interactively pick a preset region or draw/click a custom bounding box on the map. You can also open the main application (index.html), draw a bounding box anywhere on earth, and select Air Quality / Atmosphere metrics (AOT, SPDI, PM2.5 proxy, etc.) from the Primary Mode dropdown!

1. Local Area Selector & Spatial Air Quality Viewer

Choose a preset local monitoring area or interactively select any custom area on the map. The module will simulate and render high-resolution atmospheric layers (Aerosol Optical Thickness, Smoke/Plume Index, or PM2.5 Screening Proxy) directly onto the selected local bounding box.
